The process works. The plumbing doesn't.

Goals & non-goals

More accurate, faster, better owned than the incumbent.

The pilot succeeds if LaneSight gives OAG a better daily worklist than the incumbent equity tool at 3 rooftops.

Goals

  • Unify up to 8 sources into one service-to-sales model in Snowflake
  • Resolve customers into households with a garage of every known vehicle
  • Daily ranked worklist with priority tiers and reason codes
  • Recompute equity from contract and repair-order facts
  • Capture every outreach and outcome
  • Benchmark vs. the incumbent; inform pull, rebuild or retire

Non-goals for the pilot

  • Automated appraisals, offers or messages without approval
  • Replacing the DMS, CRM or service-lane tool
  • Writing back to source systems (read-only)
  • Rooftops beyond the 3 in scope

Disposition model · closed loop

One status per lead, whatever the CRM calls it.

Each CRM has its own lead statuses. LaneSight maps them to one standard lifecycle, so managers compare rooftops and owners like for like, and scoring learns from real outcomes.

New→ Contacted→ Appointment set→ Desked→ Sold / Acquired or Not nowLostDo not contact
SYNC

Read from the CRM

  • Activities, appointments and status every 15 min
  • Each CRM's codes mapped to the standard lifecycle
  • Unmapped codes flagged for review, never dropped
RULES

Manager guardrails

  • Tier 1 not contacted in 60 min → escalate to manager
  • Any Tier 1–2 lead idle 24 h → reassign prompt
  • Closing a lead requires an outcome and reason

  • Source attribution: service, CRM or OEM lead

Real-time arrival · the visit starts at the curb

Know who pulled in before the repair order opens.

Today the first signal is the RO or the equity tool's arrival email. Identifying the vehicle as it enters the drive gives sales the whole visit to work with, not what's left after the inspection.

01
0 s

Vehicle arrives

Lane camera reads the plate, or the porter or advisor scans the VIN barcode or plate with a phone.

02
target ≤ 10 s

Identify

Plate or VIN resolved to vehicle, customer and household from the golden record.

03
target ≤ 30 s

Score

Equity, lifecycle, service history and inventory fit scored; tier and reason codes assigned.

04
target ≤ 60 s

Alert the owner

Tier 1–2 → named owner and manager notified with talk track; manager pane updates live.

05
during the visit

Engage & log

Valuation pulled, conversation in the lane or waiting room, disposition logged.

Unknown vehicle?

No match in the golden record = conquest or new customer. Still scored on vehicle and inventory fit, and created as a new customer record for review.

Not on today's schedule?

Walk-ins and early arrivals are caught the same way, which the appointment-based alerts miss today.

Human in the loop

LaneSight alerts people; it never messages the customer on its own. Any automated SMS is dealer-approved and opt-in.

Timings are design targets for the pilot, to be validated in UAT.

Lane cameras (partner) Hands-free

A computer-vision partner such as SKAIVISION reads plates from the dealer's existing cameras as cars enter the drive, before an RO is written, and sends an arrival event to LaneSight.

Mobile scan (LaneSight) Any rooftop

Porters or advisors scan the door-jamb VIN barcode or snap the plate in a browser page on their phone. No app install, no new hardware. Also the fallback when a camera misreads.

Check-in event (fallback) Already there

RO open or check-in from the service-lane tool. Slower, but guarantees every visit is scored even without cameras or scans.

Integration · computer-vision partner

The camera sees the car. LaneSight knows the customer.

Scoring · starting weights, tuned in discovery

Transparent first. Learned later.

A rules-weighted 0–100 score OAG can tune. A learned model is considered only after 60 days of outcome data.

Equity: positive equity, paid off. Lifecycle: term, maturity, lease miles, warranty. Service pain: customer-pay today, repeats, failed items. Payment fit: within ±$150/mo. Engagement: web activity, recency. Acquisition need: inventory gaps, spread.
